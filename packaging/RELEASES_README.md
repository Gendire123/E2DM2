# Easy Epic Drone Movie Maker (E2DM2)

> **Drone filmmaking, simplified. From flight to epic.**
> Turn your latest drone flight recording into a polished, music-driven movie — without spending hours in an editor.

---

## 🚀 Download & Installation

E2DM2 is a local Windows desktop application. All processing is done privately on your computer.

1. **[Download E2DM2 Setup (.exe)](#)** *(Link this to your latest release setup executable)*
2. Double-click the downloaded installer and follow the instructions.
3. The installer will automatically prompt to uninstall any previous version to ensure a clean setup.

## ✨ Features

*   **Local Processing**: Your video footage stays completely local and private on your machine.
*   **Music-Synchronized Edits**: High-quality edits are generated and matched automatically to the soundtrack beats.
*   **Three-Step Workflow**:
    1.  **Import your videos**: Let E2DM2 organize your flight footage.
    2.  **Select your soundtrack**: Choose the style and energy to match the mood of the edit.
    3.  **Produce your movie**: E2DM2 outputs a polished, synced montage ready to share.

### Selection Mode: You Direct, E2DM2 Edits
Speed up your workflow without losing creative control. Paint highlights directly onto the timeline:
*   🟢 **Required**: Paint moments your final movie *must* keep (up to 20 seconds).
*   🔴 **Exclude**: Mark sections to cut from the final video.
*   E2DM2 weaves them into a cohesive story, leaving behind excluded parts.
