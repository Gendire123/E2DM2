E2DM2 v1.1.2 is the official open-source release under the MIT License!

## Highlights & Improvements

- **100% Free & Open Source:** All Pro features (custom song import, high-res export) are fully unlocked under the MIT License.
- **Updated Release Pipeline:** Official release assets are now served directly from the open-source repository `Gendire123/E2DM2`.
- **Support Development:** Added optional 'Buy Me a Coffee ☕' support link.

## Download and Installation

1. Download `E2DM2-Setup-1.1.2.exe` from the assets section below.
2. Double-click the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. The installer safely upgrades previous E2DM2 installations while preserving existing projects, presets, and media.
4. After installation, open **Help > About E2DM2** to verify version 1.1.2 is running.
