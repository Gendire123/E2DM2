E2DM2 v1.1.1 includes general performance improvements, workflow optimizations, and bug fixes for a smoother drone video editing experience.

## Highlights & Improvements

- **Workflow Enhancements:** Improved application startup and project loading responsiveness.
- **UI & Interface Polish:** Cleaned up menu structures, onboarding guidance, and dialog layouts.
- **Stability & Performance:** General bug fixes and stability improvements across video proxy generation and export pipelines.

## Download and Installation

1. Download `E2DM2-Setup-1.1.1.exe` from the assets section below.
2. Double-click the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
3. The installer safely upgrades previous E2DM2 installations while preserving existing projects, presets, and media.
4. After installation, open **Help > About E2DM2** to verify version 1.1.1 is running.
